# Review Section Guidance

## Purpose
Add social proof without making the page feel cluttered.

## Placement
Below how-it-works or above pricing.

## Initial approach
Use 3 short testimonials in clean cards.
Do not overdo stars or badges.

## Draft examples
- Fast response, easy scheduling, and the crew left everything clean.
- Best junk removal experience I have had. Clear pricing and professional service.
- They showed up on time, worked quickly, and made the whole process easy.
