# Hosting and Costs

## Preferred launch setup
- one small VPS
- Next.js app
- Postgres
- Caddy

## Why
- cheap
- predictable
- enough for early traffic
- avoids home-hosting risk

## Expected ongoing costs
### minimal starter
- VPS: roughly low single digits to low teens per month depending on provider and size
- domain: annual cost, roughly low teens per year
- email: can stay near zero at low volume on free tier
- SMS: optional and usage-based

## Cost control rules
- do not add many paid SaaS tools at launch
- self-host analytics later only if needed
- defer advanced automation until site is working
