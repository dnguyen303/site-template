# Product Brief

## Business
VetHaul is a veteran-owned junk removal service.

## Core customer promise
Fast, professional junk removal with simple booking and clear pricing.

## Target customers
- homeowners
- renters
- landlords
- property managers
- small businesses

## Positioning
Modern, premium, trustworthy local service.

## Not the brand direction
- not military-heavy
- not flag-heavy
- not rugged for the sake of rugged
- not generic stock-photo local service

## Main website goals
1. Generate trust quickly
2. Make quoting / booking feel easy
3. Support higher perceived quality and pricing power
4. Be cheap to launch and simple to evolve

## MVP pages
- Home
- Services
- Pricing
- About
- Service Area
- internal booking list page

## MVP booking flow
1. Select job type
2. Enter location / ZIP
3. Enter access details such as stairs
4. See estimate or booking request summary
5. Enter preferred date and contact info
6. Submit request

## Pricing model for MVP
- Single item: $99+
- Small load: $149+
- Medium load: $249+
- Full load: $499+
- Stairs: +$25

Use this as a starting point, not a final permanent pricing model.
